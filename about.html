About IT Care Technologies

ITC is a technology company headquartered in Karachi, Pakistan, serving organisations worldwide.

We build and implement business software (ERP, CRM, SAP, cloud, security and AI), supply and install hardware (servers, networks, CCTV, power, printing and devices) and run managed IT services. One team, one contract, one point of accountability.

Software

ERP, CRM, SAP, HR, retail, cloud, security, data and custom development.

Hardware

Computers, servers, networking, surveillance, POS, power, printing and AV.

Solutions & services

Implementation, consulting, managed IT and annual maintenance.

Industries

Sector-specific systems for retail, healthcare, education, finance and more.
